# Vision and mission

## Vision

A world in which young people can engage thoughtfully with global issues and be heard across language barriers.

## Mission

Create a real place for young people to think about the world, express ideas, share work, research issues, and communicate across languages.

## Current mission statement

> “We exist to eradicate xenophobia and glossophobia, globally — by giving young people a real place to think about the world, and the courage to say it out loud.”

## Guiding principles

- Youth voices deserve space and attribution.
- Culture, communication, and concern guide the platform.
- Research should use sources, data, and context.
- Language should widen access, not limit whose thinking is heard.
- The project should communicate honestly about its early stage.
