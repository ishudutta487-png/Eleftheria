# Content structure

## Voice

Essays, opinion pieces, and open discussion on global issues.

## Talents

Poetry, writing, art, photography, and other creative work.

## Research

Youth-led research using sources, data, and context.

## Language Access

Translation and adaptation that help ideas reach more readers and ensure language fluency does not determine whose thinking gets heard.

## Gallery philosophy

The Gallery should present creative work with context and clear attribution, supporting the platform’s cultural and editorial character.

## Editorial principles

- Keep contributors’ ideas and context clear.
- Distinguish opinion, creative work, and research.
- Give research its sources, data, and context.
- Treat translated or adapted work transparently.

## Contributor attribution

Credit each contributor clearly. Record the original creator, the content language, and, where applicable, translator or adapter credit. Reuse permissions should be handled separately from attribution.
